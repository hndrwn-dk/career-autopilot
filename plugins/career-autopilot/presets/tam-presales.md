## Things to check in these postings

- Quota or variable pay share (OTE split). Flag when the role is quota-carrying and the profile has no quota history.
- Customer count and territory: one named account vs. a region. A single-customer role is a concentration risk; flag it.
- Team size in the region. "First hire in APAC" or a very small team means broad scope and long hours; flag it, do not exclude.
- Travel percentage.
- Whether the advertised title matches the described duties (TAM vs. support vs. sales). Mismatch → flag.

## Domain signals worth credit

Enterprise support/escalation management, RCA ownership, customer training, renewals influenced, banking/telco/government customers.
