## Skill groups

- Standards: IFRS, US GAAP, local GAAP, SOX
- Reporting: month-end and year-end close, consolidation, management reporting, statutory accounts
- Planning: budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, financial modelling
- Systems: SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Xero, QuickBooks, Excel, Power BI
- Audit and control: internal audit, external audit liaison, internal controls, risk

## Credential equivalents

CPA, CA, ACCA, CIMA → professional accounting qualification · CIA → internal audit · CFA → investment analysis

## Things to check in these postings

- Whether a local qualification or registration is required to sign off accounts in that country.
- Industry-specific standards (banking, insurance, public sector).
