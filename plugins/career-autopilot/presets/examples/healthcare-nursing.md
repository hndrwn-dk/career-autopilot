## Skill groups

- Specialty: ICU, emergency, operating theatre, medical-surgical, paediatrics, oncology, aged care, community
- Clinical: patient assessment, medication administration, wound care, infection control
- Leadership: shift charge, rostering, clinical education, quality and audit

## Credential equivalents

National nursing registration → licence to practise in that country · BLS / ACLS / PALS → life-support certification · specialty post-basic certificates → specialty

## Things to check in these postings

- Registration with the target country's nursing board; flag when a conversion exam or supervised practice is needed.
- Shift pattern (nights, rotating) and ward specialty.
- Language requirement for patient communication.
