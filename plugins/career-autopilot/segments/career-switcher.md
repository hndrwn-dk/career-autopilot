# Segment: career-switcher

## Profile
- `target_field` and the reason in the user's words (optional).
- Transferable skills mapped from past roles: map each to the target field's skill groups and record the mapping for the user to confirm.
- Courses, certificates, bootcamps and projects in the new field.

## Scoring
`must_have:40, transferable:20, stack:15, certs:15, scope:10`.
- transferable: past responsibilities that match the posting's duties even under different titles.
- Count years in the old field at half weight for the new field, and say so.

## Sources
- Entry and mid-level roles in the target field; roles that bridge both fields (e.g. nurse → health-tech customer success; teacher → instructional designer).

## Filters defaults
| Key | Value |
|---|---|
| seniority | entry, junior, mid |
| min_match | 65 |

## Writing
Open with the link between the old and new field in one sentence, then the strongest transferable result and the new-field training.

## Checks
- Pay may drop when switching: compare with the user's floor and say so plainly.
